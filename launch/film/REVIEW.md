# Review: Round House launch film

**Creative calls.** I followed the person's five reference films (Burnwe 10Web and Opti, Innovid NIVO, Addx Texto, Zelios Supahub)
instead of pitching storylines: light grounds with one dark contrast world, the real UI shown large, a cursor whose click causes the
next shot, two-to-four-word lines, a tech-house bed. The film's spine is one founder's week: the old way (more tools), the hero,
one inquiry running through the workflow, Diagnose/Build/Run, the hours calculator as the payoff, the site's statement, and Let's talk.
Every UI is the site's own DOM and CSS. Every number (8.7 → 43.3 hours, $346 → $1,732) comes from the site's formula
(hours × % × 4.33, × $40). There is no voiceover. The person can add one with ElevenLabs, which has a free tier.

**Measured.** `check` passes with 0 errors. The review sheets (scene starts, middles, every cut, phone width) were opened and fixed:
- Hero UI no longer pops on at the cut.
- The approach title no longer crops during the push.
- The S3 dark title no longer shows a stray link.
- The S5 framing now holds both the title and the card.
- "Let's talk." kept its space.
- The end-card full stop is now measured while the scene is laid out.

Audio, measured on the render and not heard:
- −15 LUFS integrated, −1.2 dBTP, no clipping (two-pass loudnorm; the raw mix was −21).
- The music enters at track 9.915 s, so its drop lands at 5.88 s, on the hero.
- Every SFX is placed by its measured peak.

**Weakest shot.** Shot 4 (Diagnose/Build/Run). The UI copy is small (13–15 px at 1×, about 17 px after the push), so it reads as
texture rather than text. With more time I would punch in to 1.6× on each row as it opens.

**Warnings kept on purpose.**
- `text_below_floor`: the site's own small labels at its real scale. The film shows the real page, and the large type carries the message.
- `line_unread`: site body paragraphs (Diagnose/Build copy, the calculator note). They are context, not lines the viewer must read.

**Licences.** Music is "House Vibez" (Lily J) and the effects are Mixkit, all under Mixkit free licences, from video-shotcraft's bundle.
Re-check `pop.mp3` and `riser-cine.mp3` before paid use; that bundle records no source URL for them.
