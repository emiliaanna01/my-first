GOOD TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

01

Where should we start?

Start with Diagnose if the priority is unclear. We map the workflow, look at data and tools, and identify a practical first step. If you already have a well-defined brief, we can scope a Build directly.

02

Do we need to replace our current tools?

Usually, the useful first step is understanding the tools you already have. A tool-spend audit can reveal overlap, and a build can connect your existing CRM, inbox, forms, and documents where supported integrations allow it.

03

How do you approach AI and sensitive data?

Define approved data, access, providers, retention, and human review before building. Keep sensitive information out of an initial inquiry. An NDA and project-specific data-handling terms should be agreed before sharing confidential systems or documents.

04

How does working across time zones work?

US business-hour overlap, the primary contact, response expectations, and a meeting cadence are agreed in your proposal. There is no need for the whole team to be online all day for progress to stay visible.
