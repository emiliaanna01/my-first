# RoundHouse launch video: plan

**Input:** `RoundHouse_1.html`, a single-page site for RoundHouse, an independent digital agency.
**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, **54s** (the user asked for 40–60s, longer than brag's usual 15–25s).
**Engine:** Remotion (React) in `composition/`. The soundtrack is synthesized in `work/music.py`.

## Answers
- **What is it?** An independent digital agency that builds brands, websites and automated workflows for growing businesses.
- **Who is it for?** Growing businesses that want customers to see them more clearly and want less repetitive work behind the scenes.
- **What sets it apart?** One studio connects "how you show up" (brand and web) with "how the work gets done" (automation and AI). The work is grounded and human: "Your team handles exceptions and the decisions that need a person."
- **Most impressive claim:** the capacity calculator: *43.3 potential hours freed per month* (the site's default example, which the video labels as an illustrative estimate, just as the site does).
- **Visual hook:** the huge tight headline "Good ideas. Made real." with the lime highlight under *real*, next to the floating cluster of glossy spheres.
- **Real UI to show:** the customer-journey app (*Every hello. A little further.*), the internal assistant (*Good question. Here's a useful answer.*), the *form + feeling* identity card, the workflow nodes and the dark capacity calculator.
- **Tone:** `polished`, calm and confident with long holds. Every colour change is a **round** reveal that grows out of a circle, a nod to the name and the spheres.
- **Share caption:** "Good ideas. Made real. RoundHouse connects how you show up with how the work gets done: brand, websites, and automation in one studio."

## Identity (taken from the site's CSS)
Paper `#f7f7f2` · ink `#1d221e` · muted `#626a60` · sage `#7d8a67` · lime `#e0ed9b` · panels: journey `#e3e8cf`, knowledge `#e7e2f0`, brand `#e7dacb`, calculator `#1f251f`.
Fonts: **Inter Tight Variable** (headlines, weight 550, tight tracking) and **DM Sans** (body and eyebrows), both extracted from the page.

## Music
120 BPM, so a beat is 15 frames and a bar is 60 frames. D major, warm electric-piano chords (Dmaj9 → Bm9 → Gmaj9 → A6sus), soft kick, brushed shaker, round sub bass and a bell motif. Two bars of intro with pads only, then the groove comes in at the reveal (4s). The calculator gets a breakdown, the CTA a resolve and a ring-out. The SFX are tuned notes (D/A bells) and soft filtered air, all on the grid.
