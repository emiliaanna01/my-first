Bangladesh Land Check Atlas

Before you buy land, check four things yourself: who the record says owns it, where its boundaries are, whether a case or claim is pending on it, and whether it has already been sold. This page lists the official portals and other sources for each check. It also has a map of all 544 upazilas and a tool that measures a surveyed plot so you can compare it with the deed.

About plot-level owner maps. No public, downloadable dataset shows every plot (dag) in Bangladesh with its owner. Owner names are in khatians that you look up one mouza at a time. Plot outlines are on scanned mouza maps (CS/SA/RS/BS) or the new digital BDS survey. The official ways to see a single plot are the Smart Bhumi Naksha app, map.land.gov.bd, DLRMS and the DLRS map store, all listed below. This atlas takes you to the right upazila and shows the exact menu path to enter there.
1 · Locate

Upazila map

Drag to pan and scroll or use +/− to zoom. Click an upazila to see the selection path for the land portals and a link to satellite imagery of that spot.

2 · Measure

Plot area checker

Ask your surveyor (amin) for the GPS corners of the plot, or walk the boundary with a phone GPS app and export it. Paste the corners here. The tool measures the area in local units, compares it with the deed, finds the upazila and draws the plot on the map.

Unit sizes used: 1 decimal = 435.6 sq ft; 1 katha = 720 sq ft (the standard used in Dhaka and most registries); 1 bigha = 20 katha = 33 decimal; 1 acre = 100 decimal. Some districts use a different local katha or bigha, so confirm with the sub-registry.

Where to check, and who runs it
