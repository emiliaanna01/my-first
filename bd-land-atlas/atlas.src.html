Bangladesh Land Check Atlas

Before you buy land, check four things yourself: who the record says owns it, where its boundaries are, whether a case or claim is pending on it, and whether it has already been sold. This page lists the official portals and other sources for each check. It also has a map of all 544 upazilas and a tool that measures a surveyed plot so you can compare it with the deed.

About plot-level owner maps. No public, downloadable dataset shows every plot (dag) in Bangladesh with its owner. Owner names are in khatians that you look up one mouza at a time. Plot outlines are on scanned mouza maps (CS/SA/RS/BS) or the new digital BDS survey. The official ways to see a single plot are the Smart Bhumi Naksha app, map.land.gov.bd, DLRMS and the DLRS map store, all listed below. This atlas takes you to the right upazila and shows the exact menu path to enter there.

Gazipur district (গাজীপুর), checked on the portals

Gazipur has five upazilas and about 800 mouzas on the land record system, and much of it sits inside Gazipur City Corporation. Fast industrial growth, old Bhawal estate records and the forest land around it make Gazipur one of the riskiest districts in the country to buy land in. Click a row to show that upazila on the map.

Forest land recorded to the Forest Department

Large parts of the old Bhawal (Vawal) Court of Wards Estate were declared forest by gazette notifications in 1934, 1955 and 1984. Many RS khatians now name the Forest Department even where the CS khatian names a private owner. A seller can show genuine old papers for land the government still claims, and the High Court has seen many such suits. The Forest Department has listed more than 1,000 acres it says were grabbed with fake papers.

Check: compare the CS, SA and RS khatians for the dag. If any record names বন বিভাগ (Forest Department) or the dag is near sal forest, ask the Divisional Forest Office in Gazipur in writing before you pay. Sources: First Appeal 488/2019, Civil Revision 2760/2017, Daily Star.

Land grabbing and false cases

In August 2026, residents of Kaliganj protested that a politically connected group was taking land, demanding money and filing false cases. The same month a claimant in Outpara, inside the city corporation, alleged that false criminal cases were being used to take ancestral land. Both are allegations reported in the press. They show why a pending case search matters here.

Check: civil and criminal cause lists for the seller's name; ask neighbours who is in physical possession. Sources: Observer, 30 Aug 2026, Observer, 3 Aug 2026.

City corporation costs and building rules

Registration in Gazipur City Corporation falls in the highest cost tier, roughly 10–14% of deed value in total. Guides disagree on the exact gain tax rate (8% or Tk 80,000 per katha, against a reduced 6% or Tk 50,000), so get the figure from the sub-registry. Whether RAJUK's Detailed Area Plan covers Gazipur is still unclear. One report says the revised plan excludes Gazipur, and on 22 Oct 2026 RAJUK's own DAP page was empty. The Gazipur Development Authority (gda.gov.bd) issues land-use clearance.

Check: the deed value and fees at the sub-registry; a land-use clearance from GDA before you buy to build. Sources: fee guide 2026, UNB on DAP.

Open acquisition notice: Pubail–Dhirasram

On 8 Oct 2026 the district portal published a public notice of land acquisition for the Dhirasram inland container depot and the Pubail–Dhirasram rail link. If the land is in or near the ধীরাশ্রম or পূবাইল mouzas, check the notice's schedule of dags before you pay anything. Compensation goes to the recorded owner, not to a buyer who paid after the notice.

Check: the notice on gazipur.gov.bd and larms.land.gov.bd; ask the DC office L.A. branch. Checked 22 Oct 2026.

Khatian search on DLRMS

  1. Open dlrms.land.gov.bd and stay on the সার্ভে খতিয়ান tab. You don't need to log in.
  2. Choose ঢাকা › গাজীপুর, then the upazila. Tongi properties are under টংঙ্গি রাজস্ব সার্কেল, not গাজীপুর সদর.
  3. Under খতিয়ানের ধরন choose the survey: আর এস for the current record, এস এ or সি এস for history.
  4. Pick the মৌজা, then type the খতিয়ান নং and press খুজুন. Use অধিকতর অনুসন্ধান to search by owner name (মালিকের নাম) or dag number (দাগ নং).
  5. Double-click a khatian to order a copy. A certified copy (৳120) is posted by the DC office; the QR-coded online copy also costs ৳120.

Surveys per upazila on DLRMS: Gazipur Sadar CS, SA, RS (BDS listed but empty) · Tongi circle CS, SA, RS, BDS · Kaliganj CS, SA, RS · Kapasia SA, RS · Kaliakair RS only · Sreepur RS only. Where CS or SA is missing online, get the older record from the district record room.

2 · Measure

Plot area checker

Ask your surveyor (amin) for the GPS corners of the plot, or walk the boundary with a phone GPS app and export it. Paste the corners here. The tool measures the area in local units, compares it with the deed, finds the upazila and draws the plot on the map.

Unit sizes used: 1 decimal = 435.6 sq ft; 1 katha = 720 sq ft (the standard used in Dhaka and most registries); 1 bigha = 20 katha = 33 decimal; 1 acre = 100 decimal. Some districts use a different local katha or bigha, so confirm with the sub-registry.
