How it works

  1. 01

    Connect read-only

    Point Setpoint at your historian (OPC UA, PI, or CSV export). Nothing writes back to the DCS.

  2. 02

    Score every loop

    Each loop gets a health score from variability, time in manual, valve travel and oscillation index.

  3. 03

    Retune the worst first

    Ranked list with the likely cause and suggested Kp, Ti, Td, so your controls engineer starts with evidence.
